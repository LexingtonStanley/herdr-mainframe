# Licences of what Mainframe includes

Mainframe's own work is under the MIT licence in `LICENSE`. This folder also contains copies of, and files changed from, the projects below. Over9k, herdr-agent-ask and Emergence are MIT licensed, and their notices follow. The font is under the SIL Open Font License.

| Project | Where it is here |
|---|---|
| [Over9k](https://github.com/herunan/herdr-over9k) | `mods/over9k*` unchanged; `mods/mainframe-side` and `mods/mainframe-tabs` changed from its `over9k` and `over9k-tabs`; `mods/mainframe-chrome` after its `over9k-footer` |
| [herdr-agent-ask](https://github.com/sachin-philip/herdr-agent-ask) | `mods/asks` unchanged; `engine.js`, `parse.js` and `state.js` in `mods/mainframe-tabs` |
| [Emergence](https://github.com/shoaibkhanz/emergence) | `mods/mainframe-agents`, `mods/mainframe-prs`, `mods/mainframe-usage`, changed from its `agent-graph`, `pr-review` and `usage`. The `omp.svg` its notice mentions is `mods/mainframe-usage/omp.svg` here |
| [Pixelify Sans](https://github.com/eifetx/Pixelify-Sans) | `mods/mainframe-grid/fonts/`, SIL Open Font License 1.1, in `OFL.txt` beside it |

Flockbook and herdr-nvim are not included. The layout only has places for them.
